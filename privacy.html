Privacy Policy — Ironlog

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Who we are

Ironlog ("the app") is developed and published by Aklavya Mishra ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it goes, how it is protected and how you can delete it. Contact: aklavya.mishra123@gmail.com.

The short version

Ironlog is a training log that works without an account. Everything you enter is stored on your phone. Data leaves your phone only when you turn on a feature that needs it — Google backup, Health Connect, or viewing exercise pictures. We show no ads, run no analytics or crash tracking, and never sell or rent your data.

Data the app handles

  • Profile: first name (optional), birth year, sex, height, body weight and goal weight, optional body measurements and strength baselines, training goal and experience.
  • Training: routines, workouts, sets, weights, reps, notes and personal records.
  • Daily habits: step counts (read from Health Connect, if you connect it), water intake and your reminder schedule (wake-up and bedtime).
  • Account (only if you sign in with Google): your Google account name, email address, profile picture link and a Firebase user ID.
  • Technical data: when an online feature is used, the servers involved (Google, GitHub) receive standard connection information such as your IP address. The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or device identifiers.

How the data is used

Only to provide the app's features: building and tuning your plan, logging sessions, calculating progress, records and estimates, sending the water reminders you set up, and backing up or restoring your data when you ask. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit or insurance decisions, or to train AI models.

Where data is stored and who receives it

  • Your phone: in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Android backup: if device backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. This is controlled by you and Google, not by us.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): only if you sign in. Google acts as our service provider to verify your sign-in and store your backup. Google's servers may be located outside your country. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Health Connect: only if you connect it. It is on-device storage managed by Android.
  • GitHub: exercise pictures are downloaded from the public free-exercise-db project when you view them. These requests contain no personal data.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Health Connect

If you turn on Health Connect, Ironlog writes your finished workouts (exercise type, title, start and end time) and your weigh-ins to Health Connect. It reads your daily step totals for the past seven days, to show today's steps and your weekly trend on the Today and Progress screens, and it reads which apps recorded those steps, so Settings can tell you where your step count comes from. Data read from Health Connect is only displayed in the app; it is not saved, uploaded, included in cloud backups or shared.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to provide the features described here, is not transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people. You can change or revoke access at any time in Health Connect's settings.

How your data is protected

  • On the phone, data sits in storage protected by Android's app sandbox.
  • Everything sent over the network uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Cloud backups are encrypted at rest by Google and protected by security rules that allow only your own signed-in account to read or write them.
  • We do not look at the contents of backups. Administrative access to the service is limited to what is needed to operate it, to act on your request, or to comply with the law.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • On your phone: until you delete it with Settings → Erase everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup: kept while your account exists. Each backup replaces the previous one. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your backup and your sign-in account straight away; copies in Google's internal systems are then removed within Google Cloud's standard deletion period.
  • You can also ask for deletion without the app by emailing aklavya.mishra123@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We complete requests within 30 days.
  • Health Connect: data Ironlog wrote can be deleted in Health Connect's settings.

We keep no other copies.

Your choices and rights

You can use Ironlog without an account; sign out at any time; turn off step counting, reminders or Health Connect; edit anything you entered; export all of your data to a file; and delete your data and account as described above. Depending on where you live — for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or California law — you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to withdraw consent. Email us to exercise them; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Not medical advice

Ironlog is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Plans, calorie and distance estimates and water suggestions are general guidance only. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise programme and for any medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Children

Ironlog is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date above is updated. If a change is material, the app asks you to review and accept the new version before you continue.
