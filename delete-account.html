Delete your Ironlog account and data

Ironlog works without an account. If you signed in with Google to back up your training log, here is how to delete that account and everything stored with it.

In the app — immediate

Open Ironlog and go to You → Settings → Account & backup → Delete account. This permanently deletes your cloud backup and your sign-in account straight away. Nothing is kept.

By email — within 30 days

If you no longer have the app installed, email aklavya.mishra123@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, asking for your Ironlog account to be deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Google sign-in account for Ironlog (name, email address, profile picture link and Firebase user ID).
  • Your cloud backup: profile, routines, workouts, sets, body measurements and habit history.

Copies inside Google Cloud's internal systems are removed within Google's standard deletion period. No other copies are kept.

Data on your phone

Data stored on your own device is separate from your account. Delete it with Settings → Erase everything in the app, or by uninstalling Ironlog. Anything Ironlog wrote to Health Connect is deleted in Health Connect's own settings.

Data retention

Cloud backups are kept only while your account exists, and each backup replaces the previous one. There is no separate archive and no retention period after deletion.
